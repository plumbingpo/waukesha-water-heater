How to Tell Something Is Wrong

Catching a symptom early is often the difference between a quick part swap and a tank that gives out on the garage floor. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Rust-colored water, leaks at fittings, or moisture under the heater
  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection
  • Unsteady delivery temperatures that suggest a failing mixing valve
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life
  • Higher utility bills that point to a heater losing efficiency
  • Long waits for hot water at sinks far from the heater
  • Hot water running out during your busiest hour
  • Loud sediment noise from equipment that runs all day

Why Hire a Professional for Commercial Water Heaters?

The engineering behind commercial hot water is more demanding. Large gas inputs require confirming the supply pressure, meter size, combustion air, and vent capacity. Commercial electric units may need dedicated high-amperage circuits. High storage temperatures, commonly 140 degrees or above for sanitation, depend on thermostatic mixing valves to bring delivery down to safe levels at hand sinks and showers. Recirculation adds pumps, timers, and check and balancing valves that must be set correctly for hot water to reach every fixture on time.

Then there is the paperwork and the clock. Tips to Keep Your Water Heater Healthy

A few simple habits, like an annual flush and a quick look at the relief valve, can add years to a tank. Here are our recommendations:

  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
  • Confirm the recirculation loop still delivers hot water quickly to the far fixtures
  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
  • Schedule upgrades during slow periods instead of waiting for a breakdown
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Flush high-demand heaters on a regular interval to keep recovery strong
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
