Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a critical issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained kitchen to health inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage when a tank fails. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Waukesha Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Waukesha and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water fast, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and each response comes with full documentation for your records.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Waukesha Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Call us to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
